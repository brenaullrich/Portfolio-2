Case study

VIA Metropolitan Transit  /  Oct 2025 – Aug 2026

Making Public Transit Content People Actually Watch

RoleSocial Media & Content Specialist (agency partner) PlatformsFacebook, Instagram, X
The Challenge

VIA needed content that shared essential rider information while also engaging the broader San Antonio community, not just current riders.

What I Did

I led all short-form video, from concept to edit, and managed the content calendar, shoot coordination, trend strategy, and community management. I turned service updates and rider tips into Reels that were quick to watch and yet still informative.

About me

Hi, I’m Brena, a creative content strategist and social media content coordinator with a love for good ideas, strong visuals, and content that actually feels native to the internet.

I work across strategy and execution, turning brand goals into social-first ideas and then bringing them to life through short-form video, copy, campaigns, creator partnerships, and content production. I’m especially drawn to work that finds the sweet spot between what a brand wants to say and what people actually want to watch.

Currently, I work agency-side across a mix of brands and industries, which means I’m constantly switching between audiences, voices, trends, and creative challenges. Whether I’m building out a campaign, directing a shoot, editing a Reel, managing Influencer partnerships, or digging into performance, I like being involved from the first idea through the final post.
